Practice problems

Try a final review problem · From circuit to temperature Lectures 17 and 22 · Electrical circuits and thermal systems

A series RC circuit has R = 2 kΩ and C = 5 mF. A heated enclosure has a thermal resistance of 0.5 K/W. What thermal capacitance gives the same time constant? What fraction of a step change will both systems complete in 10 s?

Check your reasoning

The electrical time constant is 10 s. Set the thermal resistance times thermal capacitance equal to 10 s to obtain 20 J/K. Both stable first-order systems complete about 63% of their change in 10 s.
