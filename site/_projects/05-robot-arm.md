## The modeling question

How does a sliding cable change how an arm follows a desired path? Represent the closed-loop tool motion as a second-order model with virtual mass, damping, and stiffness. Use the supplied motion and experimental cable data in the coordinates specified in the handout.

## What you do

Verify the baseline, then compare tracking and timing across the prescribed cases. Explain when a simpler cable-force approximation gives a useful prediction and how the choice of virtual stiffness and damping affects tracking.
