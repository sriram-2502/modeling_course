## The modeling question

Does a tire that recovers well from a small disturbance also maintain stability in a high-speed turn? The planar model uses lateral velocity and yaw rate at a fixed forward speed.

## What you do

Compare the linear approximation with the full lateral tire curves. Test small and larger disturbances for straight-line recovery and steady cornering. Use simulated trajectories, phase portraits, and the project criteria to support a tire recommendation.
